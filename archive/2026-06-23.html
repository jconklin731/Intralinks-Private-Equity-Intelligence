Why Fundraising, Why Now

Every firm below is either raising right now or just closed a fund, which is the precise moment FundCentre and LP Onboarding earn their keep. A fresh close is not just an onboarding wave. It is the signal that the firm is already planning the next vintage, so getting in at the close is how we become the infrastructure they carry into the next fund rather than a vendor they evaluate once.
Industry context for the onboarding pain (use as framing, not as an Intralinks claim): a 2026 PE allocator outlook describes the value of GP, fund administrator, and LP all seeing exactly where each investor stands in the AML, document-sharing, subscription-agreement, and capital-transfer process simultaneously and in real time, calling onboarding "one of the most frustrating parts of a fundraise." Source: Markets Group, 2026 Private Equity Allocator Outlook.
