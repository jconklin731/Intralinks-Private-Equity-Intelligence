Expected Objections (general, role-based)
"We already have a data room provider we use deal to deal."
Most sponsors do. The question is whether that room becomes the integration and reporting system of record after close, or gets abandoned at signing and rebuilt for the next deal. The cost is the rebuild, not the license.
"Reporting is handled by our fund admin."
Fund admins produce the statement. They rarely reconcile a co-investor or continuing-holder line on a bespoke cadence. That reconciliation is the analyst-hour drain, and it sits inside the GP, not the admin.
"Not a priority mid-integration."
Integration is exactly when the reporting architecture gets set. Deciding it on a spreadsheet now is what forces the rebuild later. The window to set it cleanly is the first 90 days post-close.
